How a session works

  1. 1

    Pick your level

    A1 to C2. Not sure? Take the five-question check.

  2. 2

    Press Start

    Meet a random learner at your level, or call a friend by username.

  3. 3

    Talk with a coach beside you

    Topics, role-play cards, live captions and a chat that fixes your sentences.

  4. 4

    Practice what you missed

    A report with scores, your top mistakes and a short quiz built from them.

What the coach does during a call
